When a Drain Pushes Wastewater Back Into the House

A sewage backup happens when wastewater cannot leave the home and rises through the lowest opening it finds. In Wheat Ridge that is usually a basement floor drain, a shower stall, a laundry standpipe or a toilet on the lowest level. The cause is often a blockage in the main line, such as roots, grease, wipes or a broken section of pipe.

Many Wheat Ridge homes have basements, and the basement plumbing sits lower than the rest of the system. That makes the lowest fixtures the first place a clogged line shows itself. Older lines on established streets are prone to root intrusion.

Water of this kind is classed as Category 3, often called black water. That label is an industry term for water that carries sewage and has to be treated as contaminated.

First-Hour Safety Steps

  • Stop running water. Do not flush, do laundry or run the dishwasher.
  • Keep children, pets and anyone unwell out of the affected area.
  • Do not walk through the water or touch it without protective gear.
  • If water is near outlets, appliances or a panel, leave the area and call the utility or 911 if there is a gas smell or sparking.
  • Do not use a household wet vacuum on sewage, since it can spray droplets and contaminate the machine.
  • Call a plumber or the sewer provider to clear the line, and call us for the cleanup.

Why We Say No to Do-It-Yourself Cleanup

Category 3 water needs professional handling under industry practice, and the reason is practical. Porous materials absorb it, cleaning products only work on surfaces that are clear of soil, and a mop spreads the problem to dry rooms. Protective gear, containment and correct disposal matter more here than on any clean-water job.

How a Backup Cleanup Runs

1

Assess and contain

We trace how far the water went, close doors and seal openings so the work area stays separate from clean rooms.

2

Extract the liquid

Truck-mounted or portable extractors pull standing wastewater out before anything else moves.

3

Remove what cannot be saved

Carpet, pad, soaked drywall and insulation that touched sewage are cut out, bagged and hauled away.

4

Clean and sanitize

Concrete, framing and other hard surfaces are scrubbed clear of soil, then treated with an antimicrobial product.

5

Scrub the air and dry

Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run in the work area while air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the structure.

6

Check with meters

Moisture meters and thermal imaging confirm the area is dry, and the readings are logged.

Insurance and Sewer Backup Coverage

Coverage for sewer backup is a common surprise. Per the Denver Flood Protection Handbook, federal flood insurance leaves sewer backup out. Many homeowners policies treat backup as a separate endorsement or exclude it, so the answer depends on what you bought.

Find out what your own policy covers before work starts. Colorado Division of Insurance guidance is to call your insurer promptly, keep the loss from growing and ask for instructions before repairs begin. Your insurer decides coverage.

Sewage Backup Cleanup FAQs

Is emergency sewage backup cleanup available in Wheat Ridge?

Yes. Our line is staffed 24/7 and crews go out in call order. We cannot promise an arrival time, but we can tell you how to stay safe until the crew gets there.

Does flood insurance pay for a sewer backup?

A federal flood policy leaves out sewer backup, according to the Denver Flood Protection Handbook. Your homeowners policy may add backup coverage as an endorsement, so ask.

Can I clean up a small backup myself?

We recommend against it. Sewage water is Category 3 and needs protective gear and correct disposal. A small area still soaks into pad and drywall that look dry.

Who clears the blocked line?

A plumber or your sewer provider clears the blockage. We handle removal, sanitizing and drying once the line is open and water has stopped coming back up.

Will the smell go away?

Removing soaked porous material, sanitizing and running air scrubbers eliminates most odor. If a smell lingers, something wet is usually still in the structure.
