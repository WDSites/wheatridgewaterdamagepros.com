Why Pipes Freeze Along the Front Range

Water expands as it turns to ice, and that pressure can crack a pipe or push a joint apart. The ice itself often does not leak. The damage shows up later, when the blockage melts and the split starts pouring water into the wall.

Wheat Ridge is dry and semi-arid, but nights below freezing run from fall into late spring. A warm afternoon can follow a hard freeze and precede another one, so lines in poorly protected spaces swing between frozen and thawed. Older brick and ranch homes often have supply lines in exterior walls, crawl spaces and attached garages. If a line failed without freezing, see burst pipe water damage.

Safety Steps When a Pipe Is Frozen or Thawing

If a faucet runs dry in cold weather, assume a line is frozen and treat it carefully. Open the tap so water has a place to go as ice clears, and find the main shut-off before you try anything else.

  • Never use an open flame, torch or propane heater on a frozen pipe, since that is a fire risk behind walls.
  • Warm the area gently with a hair dryer or by raising the room temperature, starting at the faucet end and working back.
  • If water starts flowing from a wall as ice clears, close the main valve at once.
  • Keep clear of wet outlets and a wet panel. Sparks or a gas odor mean you step outside and phone 911 or the utility.
  • Leave a crack or a soft spot in a pipe to a plumber, since a thawed split looks fine until pressure returns.

Where Hidden Splits Hide

Exterior walls

Lines on the cold side of insulation freeze first, especially behind kitchen sinks and bath vanities.

Attached garages

Washer hookups and hose bibs in unheated garages get little warmth.

Crawl spaces

Vents and gaps let cold air reach pipes under the floor.

Vacant homes

A home with the heat turned down has no one to catch a leak when it thaws.

Does Insurance Cover Freeze Damage?

Many policies distinguish a sudden freeze burst from damage caused by neglect, for example a home left unheated. Ask your insurer what your policy covers and whether it requires you to keep heat on or drain the lines. We cannot say what a policy will pay.

Tell your insurer about the split quickly, and hold off on repairs until you hear back. Photographs of frost, the split and the damage are helpful. Supply-line failures are the focus of our Bel Aire page.

Prevention Before the First Hard Freeze

  • Disconnect hoses and shut off exterior bibs before fall nights drop below freezing.
  • Insulate lines in garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls.
  • Open cabinet doors on cold nights so room air reaches pipes under sinks.
  • Let a faucet drip during a hard cold snap, and keep the thermostat set for heat when you travel.

Wheat Ridge Local Note: How Long the Freeze Season Lasts

According to NWS Denver climate records, the average first freeze arrives around October 7 and the average last freeze falls around May 5. That leaves roughly seven months in which a pipe can freeze overnight.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage Questions

Is emergency frozen pipe water damage service available in Wheat Ridge?

Yes. Thaw calls come in at all hours and are taken in order. No arrival time is promised, and the phone team can walk you through the shutoff and which taps to open.

Is it safe to thaw a pipe myself?

Gentle warmth from a hair dryer on an exposed pipe is common practice. Never use a flame. If the pipe is in a wall or already leaking, close the main valve and call a plumber.

Why did the leak start only after the pipe thawed?

Ice can plug a crack. When it melts, water flows through the split under pressure. Many people discover the damage hours after temperatures rise.

What about a vacant home or second home?

Check it after a cold snap. A line that froze and split can run until someone arrives. Ask a neighbor or property manager to look, and shut off the main if you can.
