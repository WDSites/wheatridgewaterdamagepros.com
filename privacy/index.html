Privacy Policy

How we handle information from calls and from this website.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Wheat Ridge Water Damage Pros collects when you use this website or call our dispatch line. Effective date: 2026-10-28.

Calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, the address of the property and a description of the water damage so that dispatch can arrange service.

This Website

This website has no forms and does not ask you to submit personal information online. Our hosting provider may collect standard technical information such as IP address and browser type in server logs. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and Analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated.

How Information Is Used

Information from calls is used to dispatch service, communicate about your job and keep records of work performed. Records may be shared with the restoration professionals assigned to your job.

Your Choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you by calling (303) 952-8295 or emailing contact@wheatridgewaterdamagepros.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
