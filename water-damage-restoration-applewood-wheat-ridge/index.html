Services Most Applewood Calls Involve

A plumbing or appliance leak usually ends with wet drywall and flooring, and the rebuild stage is covered under water damage repair. Before a rebuild, materials must reach a dry reading, which is the job of structural drying and dehumidification.

Water that enters from above is a separate problem. A wet ceiling after a storm is explained on the roof leak water damage page, and runoff or gutter overflow that reaches the house is handled under storm water damage cleanup.

The 2017 Hail Storm and Confirmed Damage

The National Weather Service recorded 1.75 inch hail at Wheat Ridge on May 8, 2017. That was a regional storm with roof damage across a wide area. This page does not say Applewood homes were hit or spared.

First-Response Safety

  • Close the water at the valve if you can reach it without risk.
  • Keep out of standing water near outlets, cords or appliances.
  • If water is near the breaker panel or you are wet, do not touch the breakers. Call an electrician or the utility.
  • For a gas smell or sparking, get outside and call the gas utility or 911.

Sewage, known as category 3 water, calls for professional handling, and fans or DIY drying fall short.

Extraction and Drying Equipment

Crews pull standing water with extractors, then place air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. Meters and a thermal camera show what is still wet inside walls and under floors. No drying time is promised, because it depends on the materials and how far the water traveled.

Before hiring anyone for mold, find out whether your state licenses it.

Documenting the Loss for Your Insurer

Photograph and film the damage before moving anything, keep receipts, and save the failed part. The Colorado Division of Insurance advises contacting your insurer as soon as possible and limiting further damage. Their consumer line is 303-894-7490.

Applewood Water Damage Questions

How do I know I am in the Wheat Ridge Applewood?

Applewood is also a name for an unincorporated area outside the city. Check the city's neighborhood map or ask the Wheat Ridge Building Division at 303-235-2855, option 1, whether your address is inside city limits.

Is anyone available to take my call about Applewood water damage?

The line takes calls, and crews are sent in the order calls arrive. No arrival time is promised. Describe the source and where the water is, so the first steps can be explained.

Does a hail storm mean I have a roof leak?

No. Hail can damage roofs, but only a stain, a drip or wet insulation tells you water is getting in. Check ceilings and the attic.

Should I dry the room with fans myself?

For a small clean spill, fans can help. After larger losses, moisture hides in walls and floors, and only a meter reading shows materials are dry.
