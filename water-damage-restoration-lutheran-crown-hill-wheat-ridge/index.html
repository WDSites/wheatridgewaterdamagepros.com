Step by Step: From Standing Water to Dry Framing

1

Stop the source

Close the valve or isolate the leak, since drying cannot start while water arrives.

2

Extract standing water

Extractors pull water off hard floors and out of carpet and pad.

3

Map the moisture

Moisture meters check wood and drywall. A thermal camera shows cool, wet areas behind surfaces.

4

Set air movers

Air movers push air across wet surfaces so water leaves the material.

5

Run LGR dehumidifiers

Low grain refrigerant units pull that moisture out of the air so it does not settle back into materials.

6

Log and repeat

Readings are taken again and equipment is moved or removed until materials reach the dry goal.

Why Speed Matters and Why No Time Is Promised

The EPA advises drying water damage within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth. No finish time is promised, because it depends on the materials, how far water traveled and the readings. The published standard for water damage work is ANSI/IICRC S500.

If you see growth, ask whether your state licenses mold work.

Safety Before the Crew Arrives

  • Shut off the water at the valve only if it is safe to reach.
  • Stay out of standing water near electrical outlets, cords or appliances.
  • If water is near your breakers or you are wet, do not touch them. Call the utility.
  • If you smell gas or see sparking, leave and call 911 and the gas utility.
  • Sewage, called category 3 water, needs professional handling. Fans or DIY drying are not enough.

Records That Help the Insurance Conversation

Take photos before moving anything, keep receipts and keep the failed part. The Colorado Division of Insurance advises contacting your insurer early. Ask what your policy covers, since nothing here says what will be paid.

Lutheran and Crown Hill Drying Questions

Why does equipment keep running after the water is gone?

Wet framing and subfloor release moisture slowly. Removing air movers and dehumidifiers early lets that moisture settle back in, so meter readings decide when the equipment leaves.

Can wet hardwood be saved?

Sometimes. A meter shows how much moisture the boards hold, and early extraction and drying help. Boards that have cupped badly may need to be replaced.

How long does drying take after an extraction?

It varies with materials, humidity and how far water spread. Readings decide when equipment leaves, and no time is promised. Ask the crew for the numbers.
