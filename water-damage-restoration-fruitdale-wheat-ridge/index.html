Sewer Backups and Who to Call in Fruitdale

Wheat Ridge's Let's Talk effort covered 10 neighborhoods between October 2020 and September 2023, and Fruitdale was one. This page is about sewer backups and the question people ask first, who do I call. Fruitdale callers reach the same phone line shown on this site.

The Sanitation District Question

Fruitdale Sanitation District serves northwest Wheat Ridge, covering the area from Ward Road to Kipling Street and from 44th to 52nd Avenue. A home inside that area should ask the district whether the problem sits in the main or in the private service line. Verifying that your address is inside the district is up to you, and the district can confirm it.

A neighborhood map from the city shows the boundaries, so they are not restated street by street here.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your exact address.

Signs a Drain Is Backing Up

Gurgling

Sounds from a floor drain or toilet when water runs elsewhere in the house.

Rising water

Water or sewage coming up in a basement floor drain or tub.

Slow drains

Several fixtures draining slowly at the same time.

Odor

A sewage smell near drains with no visible source.

Why Category 3 Water Is Different

Sewage is category 3 water. It needs professional handling, since fans or DIY drying will not do the job. Porous materials such as carpet and drywall that sewage has touched are commonly removed rather than dried.

Safety Before Anyone Arrives

  • Stop the inflow at the valve only when that is safe.
  • Standing water near electrical stays off limits, and the breakers stay untouched if water is near them or you are wet.
  • A gas smell or sparking calls for the utility or 911.

Services for a Backup

Cleanup after sewage is covered on the sewage backup cleanup page. When the water settles in a lower level, basement water damage explains the drying. Flooding that comes from outside is handled under flood damage restoration, and general jobs belong to water damage cleanup.

Extraction and Drying Once the Source Is Clear

After the line is cleared, extractors remove standing water. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry what stays, and a moisture meter and thermal camera map where the water went. How long drying takes is not promised. If growth shows up later, mold questions should include whether your state licenses that work.

Documenting a Backup for Your Insurer

Photograph and film the room before cleanup, keep receipts and note which drains backed up. Contact your insurer early. Ask whether your policy has a sewer backup or flood add-on, since the answer varies. The state insurance division's consumer line, 303-894-7490, can explain the process.

Fruitdale Sewer Backup Questions

Do I call the sanitation district or a restoration crew first?

If you are inside Fruitdale Sanitation District, ask the district whether the main or your private line is blocked. Then call the line about the water already inside, since both steps can happen at once.

Can I be told when a crew will arrive?

Calls to the line are taken and crews go in call order. No arrival time is promised. Keep the area clear and photograph the damage while you wait.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

Category 3 water needs professional handling. Mopping or fans can spread contamination into dry areas. Stay out of the area, keep people and pets away and call.

Does home insurance cover a sewer backup?

It depends on your policy. Some include sewer backup only as an add-on. Ask your insurer directly, and keep photos and receipts before any cleanup starts.
