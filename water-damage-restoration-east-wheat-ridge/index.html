Two Kinds of Water: Outside the Building and Inside It

Rain running down a street, a storm drain that cannot keep up and a pipe that fails inside a wall are three different problems, and the cleanup differs for each. This page helps East Wheat Ridge readers work out which one they have.

East Wheat Ridge is one of the 10 neighborhoods in the City of Wheat Ridge's Let's Talk program, which ran from October 2020 to September 2023. The phone line for this site takes calls about it.

What the City Says About Street and Storm Drain Water

The city publishes a neighborhood map, and boundaries here are not restated street by street. Its floodplain information says tributaries of Sloan's Lake along 26th Avenue between Wadsworth and Sheridan flow mostly in storm sewers and streets. That is a citywide description, and this page does not say East Wheat Ridge is affected. Readers on the east side should check the FEMA map and the city for their own block.

The city also says its current flood maps took effect August 2, 2022, so an older printout may be out of date.

Which Service Page Fits

  • Water that came in from outside across a floor: flood damage restoration.
  • Rain driven intrusion and runoff: storm water damage cleanup.
  • Water pooled in a lower level: basement water damage.
  • Moisture under the floor: crawl space water damage.

Water Safety Before Anyone Arrives

Shut off the water at the valve only if it is safe to reach. Stay out of standing water near electrical outlets or panels, and leave the breakers alone if water is near them or you are wet. For a gas smell or sparking, get outside and call the utility or 911.

Water from a backed up drain or sewer is category 3 and needs professional handling. Fans and do-it-yourself drying are not enough for it.

Extraction and Drying

Crews pull standing water with extractors, then use moisture meters and a thermal camera to find wet material that cannot be seen. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the structure, with readings repeated until materials are dry. No drying time is promised. For mold, ask whether your state licenses mold work.

Documenting Before You Clean

Photograph the water and any entry point before moving things. Keep receipts and any failed part. Contact your insurer early. The Colorado Division of Insurance advises limiting further damage, getting instructions before repairs and keeping lodging receipts.

East Wheat Ridge Water Questions

Is East Wheat Ridge affected by the Sloan's Lake tributaries?

The city describes those tributaries as a citywide fact, flowing mostly in storm sewers and streets along 26th Avenue. We do not say any address here is affected. Check the FEMA map and ask the city about your block.

Does the line take calls for this neighborhood?

Yes. Calls to the line are taken, crews go in call order and no arrival time is promised. Say where the water is and what it touched.

Is storm water the same job as a burst pipe?

No. Water from outside carries soil and debris and may be category 2 or 3, while a clean supply line break is usually category 1. The category changes what can be dried and what must be removed.

Which map should I check for my own address?

Start with the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, then ask the City of Wheat Ridge about your block. The city says its current flood maps took effect August 2, 2022.
