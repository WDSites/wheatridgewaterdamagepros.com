How to Use These Guides

The posts fall into three groups. Cost and process guides explain what shapes a water damage job and what happens in what order. Ceiling, wall, wood and floor guides cover what to look for and what to save. The mold guides cover basements, appearance, removal and commercial buildings.

None of them replaces a visit. Moisture hides behind finishes, so a guide can tell you what to look for, but only readings taken in the building show where the water went. If the water is still moving or the material is already soft, calling dispatch is faster than reading.

Where the local facts come from

Each guide has a local section. The facts in it come from the City of Wheat Ridge, Jefferson County, the City of Arvada, the City of Golden, the City and County of Denver, the Colorado Division of Insurance, the EPA, FEMA and the National Weather Service. When a source did not cover a point, we sent you to the right office instead of guessing.

Reading order that works

If water is on the floor today, start with the process guide and then the guide for the material that got wet. If you are only worried, start with the signs guide, then the cost guide so you know what questions to ask when a crew arrives. Mold readers should begin with the basement mold guides and finish with the visual guide.
