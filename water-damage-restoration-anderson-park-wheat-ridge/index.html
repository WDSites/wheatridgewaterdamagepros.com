Document Before You Dispose

1

Photograph wide, then close

Take a few shots of each wet room from the doorway, then close-ups of stained walls, lifted flooring and wet furniture.

2

Keep the failed part

A cracked hose, split fitting or leaking valve is evidence. Bag it and label where it came from.

3

Hold damaged items

Do not discard soaked belongings until they are photographed and listed.

4

Save every receipt

Keep fans, towels, lodging and plumber visits in one folder.

Cleanup Services That Fit a Documented Loss

Most losses start with water damage cleanup, which covers removing standing water and wet materials. Once the building is dry, water damage repair covers rebuilding what had to come out.

A failed dishwasher or washer line is covered on the appliance leak page, and soaked flooring on carpet water damage.

First-Response Safety Before You Pick Up a Camera

  • Shut off the water at the valve only if you can reach it without stepping through water.
  • Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords or appliances.
  • If water is near the breaker panel or you are wet, leave the breakers alone and call the utility.
  • For a gas smell or sparking, get outside and call 911 and the gas utility.
  • Sewage is category 3 water. It needs professional handling, and fans or DIY drying are not enough.

How Extraction and Drying Fit Into the Record

Extractors pull standing water from floors. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry what remains, while moisture meters and a thermal camera show where water traveled. No finish time is promised. Ask for the readings, since they show what was wet and when it dried.

If mold appears, ask whether your state licenses mold work.

Talking to Your Insurer

The Colorado Division of Insurance advises contacting your insurer as soon as possible, limiting further damage, getting instructions before repairs and keeping lodging receipts. Two questions are worth asking early. One is how the policy treats a sudden loss compared with a slow, gradual one. The other is whether flood or sewer backup coverage is a separate add-on. Nothing here promises what will be paid.

Anderson Park Water Damage Questions

Can I throw away wet carpet before the insurer sees it?

Photograph and video it first, and cut a small labeled sample if you can. Ask your insurer before discarding large items. Keep receipts for anything you remove or replace.

Does the line take calls for Anderson Park?

Yes. Calls to the line are taken, and crews go in call order. No arrival time is promised, so document and make the area safe while you wait.

What should I keep from a broken appliance line?

Keep the failed hose or fitting and photograph where it was installed before anyone moves it. It helps show how the loss started and what part failed.

Is a sewer backup covered like a burst pipe?

Often not in the same way. Ask your insurer whether sewer backup is an add-on, and how the policy words sudden versus gradual loss. Answers differ by policy.
