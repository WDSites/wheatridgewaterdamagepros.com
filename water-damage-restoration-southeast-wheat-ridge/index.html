Moisture Control Comes First

The EPA's central point is that moisture control is the key to mold control. Cleaning growth off a surface while the cavity behind it stays wet only buys time. The plumbing, roof or appliance failure has to be stopped and the structure dried.

Crews dry with extractors, air movers, LGR dehumidifiers and moisture meters, plus a thermal camera to find wet areas behind surfaces, as described on the structural drying and dehumidification page. No drying time and no mold outcome is promised.

Testing, Porous Materials and Health Questions

  • The EPA says sampling is usually unnecessary when mold is visible.
  • Porous materials with growth, such as ceiling tile, carpet and drywall, are often discarded.
  • Ask whether your state licenses mold work before hiring removal. The mold remediation page explains what removal involves.
  • Questions about health effects belong with a physician.

Hidden Moisture Sources

A clogged condensate line can wet a ceiling or closet slowly, which the AC leak water damage page covers. If your building is commercial, the commercial water damage page explains scope.

Safety Before Anything Else

Shut off the water at the valve only if it is safe to reach. Stay out of standing water near electrical outlets or panels, and leave the breakers alone if water is near them or you are wet. For a gas smell or sparking, go outside and call the utility or 911. Sewage is category 3 and needs professional handling, since fans and do-it-yourself drying are not enough.

Documentation for Your Insurer

Photograph and video the damage and any visible growth before cleanup. Keep receipts and the failed part. Contact your insurer early. The Colorado Division of Insurance advises limiting further damage, getting instructions before repairs and keeping lodging receipts. Ask what your policy says about mold.

Southeast Wheat Ridge Mold and Water Questions

How fast does mold show up after a leak in Southeast Wheat Ridge?

It depends on how long materials stay wet. The EPA advises drying within 24 to 48 hours. Past that, mold becomes more likely, but no one can say exactly when it will appear in a given house.

Does the line take calls for this neighborhood?

Yes. Calls to the line are taken, crews go in call order and no arrival time is promised. Say what is wet and how long it has been so the call can be sorted.

Should I pay for mold testing?

The EPA says sampling is usually unnecessary when you can see growth. Fixing the water source and drying matter more. Ask whether your state licenses mold work if you plan to hire removal.

Can you promise mold will not return?

No. Mold returns when moisture does. Crews can dry the structure and record readings, but a promise would be false if the water source is not repaired.
