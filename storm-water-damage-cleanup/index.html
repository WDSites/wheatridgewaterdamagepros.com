What Storm Water Damage Means for a Wheat Ridge Home

Storm water damage is what a thunderstorm does to a house from the outside in. Rain pushed sideways by wind gets past siding and window frames. Downspouts spill next to the foundation. Window wells fill faster than they drain. A summer cloudburst can drop an inch of rain in under an hour, and the soil along the Front Range is dense enough to shed water toward the walls instead of absorbing it.

This page covers that wet-weather category. It is not the same as a creek or street flood, which is handled under flood damage restoration, and it is not water that comes through the roof, which is covered at roof leak water damage.

Safety Steps When Storm Water Is Inside

  • Do not wade into a flooded basement if the water is near outlets, a furnace or a panel. Electricity and water together can be fatal.
  • If the water has reached the electrical panel or you hear buzzing, stay out and call 911 or the utility.
  • Natural gas smell means leave and call the gas utility from outside.
  • Storm water carries soil, fertilizer and sewer overflow. Treat it as contaminated and wear boots and gloves.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on water, and do not run the furnace if the water has touched it.
  • Move valuables up and photograph the damage before you touch anything.

If water keeps arriving, there may be little to do until the rain stops. Once it eases, call and we will explain what can be done safely.

How We Clear and Dry After a Storm

1

Extract

Portable and truck-mounted extractors lift standing water. Sump or transfer pumps are used where depth calls for them.

2

Remove soaked material

Wet carpet pad, baseboards and low drywall are removed because storm water is treated as contaminated.

3

Clean and sanitize

Silt is rinsed off hard surfaces and antimicrobial treatment is applied to what stays.

4

Dry

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry framing and concrete. Meters and thermal imaging back up the readings.

5

Air quality

Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration help with odor and airborne dust during demolition.

Our work starts after the water source is controlled. Regrading, gutter work and window well drains are for a landscaper, a gutter contractor or a contractor you hire. Once it is dry, the final stage is water damage repair.

Summer Thunderstorms and the Hail Factor

Colorado's afternoon storms arrive fast. The National Weather Service recorded hail measuring 1.75 inches across in Wheat Ridge and Golden on May 8, 2017, a reminder that a single storm can chew up shingles, crack window glazing and tear screens, all of which become entry points for the next rain.

Insurance and Storm Water

Coverage differs between wind-driven rain, surface flooding and sewer backup, so read your policy carefully. The Colorado Division of Insurance tells owners to notify the insurer quickly and limit more damage. Ask whether a separate flood or water backup endorsement applies. Photographs and moisture logs are kept for your records, and no coverage is promised.

Wheat Ridge Local Note: What Storm Water Hits First

NWS Denver records show the average first freeze falls on October 7, so gutters and window wells are best cleared before then. Denver's Flood Protection Handbook points out that federal flood insurance leaves out sewer backup, has a 30-day wait and treats a crawlspace as a basement.

Prevention Before the Next Storm

  • Clear gutters and extend downspouts several feet from the foundation.
  • Keep window well covers clean and the drains clear of leaves.
  • Check that soil slopes away from the walls.
  • Seal visible foundation cracks and window frame caulk.
  • Confirm your sump pump runs and has a working float. See basement water damage for what happens when one fails.

EPA mold guidance gives wet materials about a day or two to dry, so a basement left alone over a weekend invites growth. If growth appears, mold remediation covers it. We refer to ANSI/IICRC S500 as the published water damage standard, not as a certification.

Storm Water Damage Cleanup FAQs

Is emergency storm water damage cleanup available in Wheat Ridge?

Yes. Calls are answered day and night. Crews go out in call order, no arrival time is promised, and a big storm can mean many calls at once.

Is this the same as flood damage?

Not quite. Storm water damage comes from rain, gutters, window wells and grading. Overland flooding from a creek or street is covered on the flood damage restoration page.

Do you fix the gutters or grading?

No. We clear water and dry the structure. Gutters, grading and window well drains are for the right contractor so the problem does not repeat.

Can I save wet carpet after a storm?

Storm water is treated as contaminated, so carpet and pad are usually removed. Hard surfaces can often be cleaned and dried instead.
