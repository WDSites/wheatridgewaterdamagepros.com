What Water Does to a Wood Floor

Wood swells across the grain when it gets wet, and boards push against each other as they take on moisture. Wheat Ridge has plenty of wood floors in older ranch and brick homes, often laid over a basement or crawl space.

Wood floors can survive water if it is removed fast and the drying is controlled. Too fast and boards can split. Too slow and they cup, stain or grow mold underneath.

Hardwood Floor Safety Steps in the First Hour

Find and stop the water first. For a supply leak, close the fixture valve or the main water valve. Wet floors are slippery, and water near outlets or baseboard heaters can be an electrical hazard. If you see sparking or smell gas, go outside and call 911 and the utility.

How Wood Floors Get Dried

1

Extract

Surface water is lifted off with an extractor and a squeegee head made for wood.

2

Measure

A pin or pinless meter checks boards against a dry reference reading, and a thermal camera maps wet zones.

3

Set drying mats

Drying mats with suction pull moisture from the board surface and from below through small seams.

4

Add air and dehumidification

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers keep the room dry so the wood has somewhere to release moisture.

5

Re-read daily

Boards are measured again at each visit. Drying stops when readings match the dry reference.

When Boards Come Up

Sometimes the only way to dry the subfloor is to remove a few boards. A row at the edge or a short run in the middle gives air access to wet plywood and insulation underneath. It is a controlled opening, done only where readings call for it.

Moisture Content Readings and Why They Matter

A meter reading is the only honest way to know if a wood floor is dry. Wood in a stable, heated Colorado home settles to a lower moisture content than the same wood in a humid climate, so the reading is compared to a reference taken from an unaffected area of the same floor. The drying goal is that match, not a number from a chart.

We log those readings. A written record helps the flooring contractor decide when the floor is ready for refinishing, and it is useful if an insurer asks.

Solid Versus Engineered Hardwood

Solid hardwood

Cuts and swells with moisture but can often be dried and sanded later. Thick boards tolerate more drying time.

Engineered hardwood

Layers can separate when soaked. Thin top layers limit how many times it can be refinished.

Floating floors

Water can pool under the whole floor, so a larger area may be lifted to dry the subfloor.

Over a Basement or Crawl Space

A floor above a basement or crawl space can get wet from above or below. A leaking water heater, a pipe in a joist bay or damp air from a wet crawl space can all lift a floor with no spill on top. If the subfloor is wet from below, address that first. Our crawl space water damage and basement water damage pages cover those spaces.

Sanding and refinishing is a flooring trade step, not part of drying. We dry and document. A flooring contractor decides when the floor is flat and dry enough to sand and coat.

Wheat Ridge Local Note: Dry Winters and Wood

Furnaces run through much of the year here, and dry heated air is hard on wood that has already been wet.

Heating dries indoor air, and wood floors move with it, which is why a floor can look different in February than in July even before any water.

The city building division, at 303-235-2855, can say whether replacing a floor or subfloor needs a permit.

What Changes the Cost of Floor Drying

  • The wood type and thickness, solid or engineered.
  • Whether the water was clean or contaminated.
  • The number of mats and panels needed, and how long they run.
  • Refinishing or replacement, which a flooring trade prices separately.

Insurance and Standards

Wood floor claims turn on policy wording, so ask the insurer how it treats a burst line versus a seep. Colorado's insurance division recommends holding off on repairs until the carrier gives instructions. ANSI/IICRC S500 is a published drying reference.

Hardwood Floor Water Damage FAQs

Is emergency hardwood floor water damage repair available in Wheat Ridge?

Yes. Calls are taken at any hour and crews are sent in the order calls arrive. No arrival time is promised, so phone as soon as boards get wet.

Will my hardwood floor cup if it gets wet?

Cupping is common but not certain. Fast extraction and controlled drying can limit it. Many cupped floors flatten once moisture readings return to normal.

Can you sand and refinish after drying?

Sanding and refinishing is a flooring trade step. We dry the floor and record readings, then a flooring contractor decides when to sand and finish.

How do you know the floor is dry?

A moisture meter is used on the boards and compared with a dry reference from an unaffected area. Readings are repeated until they match.

Do boards always have to be pulled up?

No. Mats and panels dry many floors in place. Boards are lifted only when the subfloor stays wet or the floor has buckled.
