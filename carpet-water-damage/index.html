Wet Carpet Is Really Two Problems

Carpet has two layers that behave differently. The top is fiber and backing, which can often be cleaned and dried. Under it sits the pad, a foam or rebond sponge that soaks up water and holds it long after the surface feels normal. Most of the decisions in a wet carpet job come down to the pad, the water, and how long both stayed wet.

In Wheat Ridge, wet carpet shows up after a failed water heater, a washing machine hose, a toilet overflow, a roof leak or a basement seep. Carpet is common in bedrooms, family rooms and finished lower levels, so it is one of the most frequent materials in a water loss. The speed of the call matters more than the size of the spill.

Is the Water Clean, Gray or Contaminated?

Water category decides whether carpet is saved or discarded. The industry terms are Category 1 for clean water from a supply line, Category 2 for gray water such as a washing machine or dishwasher drain, and Category 3 for black water such as sewage or river water. Category 3 needs professional handling.

Wet Carpet Safety Steps in the First Hour

Find the source and close the valve first. For a fixture, use the shut-off under it. For a broken line, close the main water valve. A wet carpet over a slab or plywood is an electrical risk if cords, power strips or baseboard heaters sit in it, so keep breakers off for the affected circuit if water reached outlets.

  • Do not walk through wet areas more than you need to, since foot traffic pushes water into the pad.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet, because of the shock risk.
  • If you smell gas or see sparks, get outside and ring 911 and the gas company.

How We Extract and Dry Carpet

1

Extract

A truck-mounted or portable extractor pulls water from the carpet and the pad. Repeated passes remove far more than a wet vac.

2

Check the pad and tack strip

The pad is tested and the carpet edge is peeled back at a corner to see the tack strip and the subfloor.

3

Float the carpet

Where the carpet can be saved, it is lifted and supported off the pad so air moves under it.

4

Dry with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers

Air movers push air under the carpet while dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the room.

5

Verify

Meters check the carpet, the subfloor and any concrete, and a thermal camera looks for damp spots.

Subfloor and Concrete Under the Carpet

What sits under the pad matters. Plywood and OSB can swell and delaminate. Concrete holds water and releases it slowly, which is why a carpet that looks dry can feel damp days later. Both are measured with meters before new pad or carpet goes down.

Odor and Why It Lingers

Odor means material is still wet or something grew in it. Cleaning the surface does not fix it. The fix is removing wet pad, drying the subfloor and, in some cases, using an air scrubber or deodorizing treatment. If a smell persists after drying, ask for another reading in case moisture is hidden.

Stretching Carpet Back Into Place

A carpet that was floated and dried often needs to be re-stretched to lie flat again. Re-stretching is done with a power stretcher and re-attached to the tack strip, with new pad underneath. It is a flooring trade step and normally follows drying. A flooring installer or carpet repair company is the right call for it.

Upstairs Carpet Versus Basement Carpet

Main floor and upstairs

Water often came from a fixture or ceiling. Drying is usually room by room, and the downstairs ceiling is checked.

Bedroom over a finished basement

Water can travel down through the floor, so the room below may be wet too.

Basement carpet on concrete

Concrete stays damp longer, and glue-down carpet is harder to float.

The 2020 Census counted 32,398 people in Wheat Ridge, and carpet is common in the finished lower levels of many homes.

Sewer service in part of the city comes from a sanitation district, which tells you who to call if wastewater reached the carpet.

If sewage reached your carpet, the water is Category 3, and your sewer provider or a plumber must clear the line before cleanup starts.

What Changes the Cost

  • Water category, since black water adds removal and disposal.
  • Square footage of carpet and pad.
  • Subfloor type and whether it needs drying or replacement.
  • Re-stretching or new carpet, which is priced separately.

Carpet Water Damage FAQs

Is emergency carpet water damage restoration available in Wheat Ridge?

Yes. Someone answers at every hour and crews head out in call order, with no promised arrival time. Call right away about the wet pad.

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often yes, if the water was clean and the carpet is dried within a day or two. Contaminated water or long wet time usually means removal.

Do I have to replace the pad?

Usually. Pad acts like a sponge and is inexpensive compared with the carpet. Replacing it is the most reliable way to remove trapped water and odor.

How long does wet carpet take to dry?

It depends on the carpet, the pad, the subfloor and the humidity. Readings guide the answer, and equipment stays until the meters say it is dry.

Why does the room still smell after it feels dry?

Moisture can remain in the pad, tack strip or concrete. Ask for another reading, and treat a lasting smell as a sign something is still wet.
